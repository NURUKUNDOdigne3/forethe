Who we are
Our story

A company built in the forest, not in an office

Forêt H.E Inc. was founded on a simple conviction: good forestry is done by people who know the ground they stand on. From our base in Gatineau, we deliver silvicultural treatments for public forests, private woodlots, and Hydro-Québec mandates across Québec and Ontario.

Every contract we take on starts the same way — boots on the site, an honest assessment of what the stand needs, and a plan that serves the forest's long-term health rather than the shortest route to a finished invoice.

Our crews are trained, certified, and equipped for demanding terrain. That combination of skill and care is what keeps landowners and forest managers coming back to us season after season.

What drives us

Mission, vision & values

Our mission

To deliver professional forestry management services that improve the health, productivity, and long-term value of every stand we touch — carried out safely, on schedule, and to the standard we would want on our own land.

Our vision

To be the forestry contractor of choice in the Outaouais and beyond — recognised not for the size of our fleet, but for the quality of our work, the safety of our crews, and the forests we leave better than we found them.

Our values

Safety before speed. Honesty in every quote and every report. Respect for the land, for our clients, and for the people who do the work. And a stubborn commitment to finishing what we start, properly.

Why work with us

What sets our crews apart

Forestry contracts live or die on execution. Here is what you can count on when Forêt H.E Inc. is on your site.

  • Certified, trained crews

    Every operator is certified for the equipment they run, with safety protocols applied on every site, every day.

  • Prescription-based work

    We work to a written intervention plan, not guesswork — so you know exactly what was done and why.

  • On schedule, in season

    Each project closes with a completion report — photos, mapped areas, and recommendations for what comes next.

The people behind the work

Our team

Forestry is a team discipline. Ours brings together planning, field operations, and safety training — each one essential to a treatment done right.

Field operations

The crews

Certified brushcutter and mulcher operators, planters, and thinning specialists. They carry out the treatment on the ground — in steep terrain, dense stands, and full summer heat — to the specification set out in the plan.

Silviculture & planning

The planners

They walk the site before anyone else does: assessing stand density, species composition, and regeneration, then writing the prescription that tells the crew exactly what to cut, what to keep, and why.

Safety & training

The standard-keepers

Responsible for certification, equipment checks, and the daily briefings that keep our record clean. No crew starts a shift without a safety plan for that specific site and that specific day.

Interested in joining a crew? Get in touch — we are always glad to hear from skilled forestry workers.
